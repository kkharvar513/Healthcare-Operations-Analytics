# Healthcare Operations & Patient Management Analytics

## Project Overview

This project uses Tableau to analyze healthcare operations, patient appointments, treatment performance, and billing information. It consists of two interactive dashboards designed to help users explore operational performance and identify areas that may require management attention.

## Objectives

- Monitor patient and appointment activity using KPI cards.
- Analyze monthly appointment trends and appointment outcomes.
- Compare appointments across medical specializations.
- Evaluate billing amounts by payment method and treatment type.
- Explore treatment cost variation.
- Identify operational patterns using an appointment activity heatmap.
- Compare financial metrics through interactive parameters.

## Tools & Technologies

- Tableau
- Calculated Fields
- Parameters
- Data Relationships
- Data Visualization
- Exploratory Data Analysis

## Dashboards

### Dashboard 1: Healthcare Operations Analytics

This dashboard provides an overview of patient numbers, appointments, appointment status, appointment outcomes, monthly trends, and appointment activity.

### Dashboard 2: Healthcare Financial & Treatment Analysis

This dashboard focuses on treatment-wise billing, payment methods, treatment cost distributions, and dynamic financial analysis.

[**View Dashboard 2 on Tableau Public**](https://public.tableau.com/views/Healthcare_Operations_Analytics/Dashboard2?:showVizHome=no)

## Data Modeling

The Tableau data model connects the Patients, Appointments, Doctors, Treatments, and Billing tables using relationships to support analysis across healthcare operations.

## Key Features

- KPI cards for total patients, total appointments, total billing amount, and total paid amount.
- Monthly appointment trend analysis.
- Appointment status and outcome distribution.
- Appointments by medical specialization.
- Revenue by treatment type.
- Billing amount by payment method.
- Appointment activity heatmap.
- Treatment cost box plot.
- Dynamic financial analysis using a parameter.

## Calculated Fields

The project includes calculated fields for:

- Treatment Profit
- Profit Margin %
- Appointment Outcome

A parameter-driven calculated field allows users to switch between Billing Amount, Treatment Cost, and Appointment Count.

## Key Insights

- Out of 200 appointments, 52 were recorded as no-shows and 51 as cancelled.
- April recorded the highest monthly appointment count, at 25, while September recorded the lowest, at 11.
- Chemotherapy had the highest billing amount among the displayed treatment types, at ₹128,856.
- Credit card payments accounted for ₹201,382 in billing, followed by insurance and cash.
- The appointment activity heatmap helps identify busier and quieter day-month combinations.
- Treatment cost distributions differ across treatment types.

*These insights reflect the dataset used in this project and should not be interpreted as general conclusions about all healthcare facilities.*
